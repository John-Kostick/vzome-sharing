Some more unconventional hyperboloids.  Using a graphical technique, hyperboloids can be generated in various symmetries. The first shown has indicated facetted hyperboloid surfaces bounded by a cube frame.

This is a sequel to [Constructions of Hyperboloids with Grids](https://john-kostick.github.io/vzome-sharing/2022/08/02/Phi-hyperboloid-6-10-20-19.html)
