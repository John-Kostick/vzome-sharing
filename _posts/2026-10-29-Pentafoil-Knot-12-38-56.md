This is a version of Pentafoil Knot construction. It is composed in a way that can serve as a model for building in wood, possibly as a table base.  This takes inspiration from [Trefoil Knot Table](https://john-kostick.github.io/vzome-sharing/2022/12/22/Trefoil-Knot-Table-15-51-47.html)

The particular method used in this construction is explained in [Tubular Edge Constructions of Polyhedra](https://john-kostick.github.io/vzome-sharing/2025/01/15/Oct-tet-constructions-14-08-40.html)

Scenes shows some features, such as the cross-section profile of the sticks, which greatly facilitates cutting of mitre joints.
